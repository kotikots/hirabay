HiraBay

🛍️ Welcome to HiraBay — A Marketplace Made for NEUST Students!
HiraBay is your go-to platform for buying, selling, and borrowing items within the Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology (NEUST) community.
Whether you're looking for affordable textbooks, gently used uniforms, gadgets, or just want to lend or borrow school supplies — HiraBay connects students to help each other out. It's simple, secure, and student-centered!
It provides a safe and organized space where students can exchange goods such as books, uniforms, gadgets, and school supplies, promoting affordability, sustainability, and peer collaboration.
